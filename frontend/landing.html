How it works

From signal to decision in three steps

TrendScope handles the entire intelligence pipeline — you get actionable output without managing data pipelines or writing queries.

1

Connect data sources

Point TrendScope at your categories of interest. It continuously ingests signals from web, market, and behavioural data streams without any manual configuration.

2

AI analyzes trends

The analysis engine scores each signal for strength, velocity, and credibility — then clusters related signals into coherent trends with lifecycle tracking.

3

Get actionable intelligence

Receive structured reports, drift alerts, and buy/hold/sell signals via the dashboard or API. Act on intelligence your competitors don’t have yet.
